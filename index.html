        Settings

        The 3-minute rule. During a focus block, if you leave the app (close it, change tabs, lock the phone) for 3 minutes or more, the matter drifts off and the star does not form. A pause also holds for only 3 minutes. Breaks have no limit. If you stop a timer block yourself after 5 minutes or more, you get a brown dwarf. A stopwatch block needs 5 minutes or more to form a star.
        Timer ring

        With the 60-minute ring, a 10-minute block fills one sixth of the ring, so you see real time. "Fit" fills the full ring for every block.
